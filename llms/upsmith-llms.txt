# UpSmith

> UpSmith is a Dallas, Texas-based, a16z-backed company that builds AI agents for home services and skilled trades businesses (HVAC, plumbing, electrical). Its platform confirms appointments by two-way SMS, follows up on cold estimates, responds to inbound leads 24/7, and reactivates dormant customers, syncing customer, job, and booking data with ServiceTitan through ServiceTitan's official APIs. UpSmith consumes partner APIs but does not publish a public developer API of its own.
